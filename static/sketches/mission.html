Mission

Our Vision

To support human-machine collaboration and co-evolution by making machine intelligence explainable, transparent, fair and understandable to human decision-makers.

Our Mission

  1. Balanced
    • Accuracy
    • Efficiency
    • Complexity
    • Interpretability
    01

    Design fit-for-purpose machine intelligence

    that is driven by real-world problems, grounded in domain principles, and optimised to achieve the best balance among accuracy, efficiency, complexity, interpretability, and other task-specific requirements.

    1. Data
    2. Features
    3. Model
    4. Prediction
    5. Decision
    02

    Embed explainability and interpretability by design

    throughout the machine intelligence pipeline, from data and feature representation, model development, prediction and decision-making, to develop transparent, accountable and trustworthy AI systems.

  3. Explainable AI
    • Healthcare
    • Logistics
    • Workforce
    • Energy
    03

    Deliver explainable machine intelligence across domains

    translating methodological advances into practical, innovative and sustainable solutions for real-world challenges.
