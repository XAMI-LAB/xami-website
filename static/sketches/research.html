01

Explainable and Trustworthy Intelligent Systems

We advance the development of explainable, interpretable and trustworthy intelligent systems through innovative approaches to understanding, evaluating and improving machine intelligence.

Our research spans explainable AI, counterfactual explanations, model transparency, adversarial robustness and systematic evaluation, with a focus on making intelligent systems more transparent, reliable, robust and accountable in real-world applications.
