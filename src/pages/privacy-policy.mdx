# Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information is handled when you visit the XAMI Lab website. The site is a static website: it has no user accounts, no forms, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Hosting

The website is hosted on [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/). Like any web host, Cloudflare receives standard request information (such as IP address, browser user agent, requested page, and time of request) when your browser loads a page. This information is processed by Cloudflare under its [privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/). XAMI Lab does not use it to identify visitors.

## Cookies and Local Storage

This website does not set cookies. Your light or dark theme preference is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it on your next visit. It never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Content

Some pages load content from third-party services, which receive your IP address and browser information when that content is requested:

- **Member photos** are served from [jsDelivr](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net).
- **XAMI-Tube** shows video thumbnails loaded from YouTube. A video player is only loaded after you click play, and it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (`youtube-nocookie.com`). Playback is subject to [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Footer badges** are loaded from [docusaurus.io](https://docusaurus.io/).

Links to external websites (such as university profiles, LinkedIn, or GitHub) are governed by those websites' own privacy policies.

## Contact

If you email us, your email address is used only to reply to you.

For questions about this policy, email [xami.initiaive@gmail.com](mailto:xami.initiaive@gmail.com).

## Changes to This Policy

This policy is effective as of 26 September 2026. Any changes will be posted on this page.
